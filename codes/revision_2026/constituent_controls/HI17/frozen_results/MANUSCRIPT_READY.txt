DESIGN
The hydrologically informed framework contains 18 cluster-conditioned selection slots and 17 unique regionally trained configurations. Constituent performance is evaluated using every archived final realization available within the prespecified 10-seed protocol; no missing realization is imputed or replaced. Pooled-HI-versus-constituent comparisons use exactly the same final-seed subset available for that constituent. HI-CFG-01...HI-CFG-17 are arbitrary source-token-sorted identifiers, NOT validation ranks.

PRIMARY AGGREGATION
Metrics are calculated per basin x seed and then averaged arithmetically across all archived final seeds available for that constituent. The frozen pooled HI ensemble is evaluated across all 10 final seeds. For each constituent-level paired comparison, pooled HI is re-aggregated over the identical seed subset used by that constituent. The raw available-member pointwise median is independently rebuilt for every basin and all 10 seeds and must match the frozen pooled ensemble before analysis proceeds.

INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
This experiment evaluates pooled hydrologically informed aggregation against its own 17 unique selected constituents within the full prespecified 10-seed protocol, using all archived final realizations and exact seed matching within each pooled-versus-constituent comparison. It tests whether pooled performance is reproduced by individual selected configurations in isolation. It does NOT isolate HPO causally, and it does NOT turn the arbitrary HI-CFG identifiers into validation ranks.
